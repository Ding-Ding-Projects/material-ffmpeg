PRIVACY

Your media stays on your machine.

material-ffmpeg is designed for local processing. Files, command settings, job output, and preferences remain on the device. There is no account, analytics, or required cloud service.

Real execution uses the FFmpeg and FFprobe binaries available to the app. The UI remains usable for planning and inspection when those tools are not present.
